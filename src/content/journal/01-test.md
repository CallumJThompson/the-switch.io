The Gateron Oil King has held its position as a default recommendation for stock linear switches since its initial release. However, community reports over the past twelve months have raised questions regarding factory lube migration and batch-to-batch variance.

To test whether current production runs match the original specification, we sourced five independent retail batches manufactured across three quarters and ran twenty switches through our load cell rig.
